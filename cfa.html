Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA)

🧑‍🎨 What Do They Do?

  • Have you heard of the share market?
  • In the share market, people buy shares of companies.
  • If the company grows, the value of the shares they bought may also increase.
  • But how do you know which company you should invest in?
  • This is where a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA)can help.
  • A CFA studies the company’s financial information, how the company is performing, its future plans, and what is happening in the market.
  • They use this information to understand whether the company could be a good investment.
  • 👉 In simple words: A CFA helps people and organisations invest their money wisely.

🎓 How to Become a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA)

  • After Class 12, you can choose a bachelor's degree in any field.
  • You can start the CFA Program during your bachelor's degree when you meet the CFA Institute eligibility requirements, or after graduation.
  • Follow the steps below to become a CFA charterholder.
STEP 1

Complete a bachelor's degree in any field.

Note: Students can also start the CFA Program while doing their bachelor's degree if they meet the eligibility requirement.

STEP 2

Register for the CFA Level I exam through the CFA Institute website.

CFA Institute Official Website
STEP 3

Pass the CFA Level I examination and complete the required Practical Skills Module. CFA Level 1 Examination

STEP 4

Pass the CFA Level II examination and complete the required Practical Skills Module. CFA Level II examination

STEP 5

Pass the CFA Level III examination and complete the required Practical Skills Module. CFA Level III examination

STEP 6

Gain 4,000 hours of relevant professional work experience over at least 36 months.

STEP 7

Apply for CFA Institute membership and become a CFA charterholder.
