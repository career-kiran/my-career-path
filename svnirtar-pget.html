SVNIRTAR PGET

SVNIRTAR PGET stands for Swami Vivekanand National Institute of Rehabilitation Training and Research Post Graduate Entrance Test.

It is an entrance examination conducted by SVNIRTAR for admission to selected postgraduate courses in Physiotherapy and Occupational Therapy.

Students who have completed the required Bachelor's degree can apply for the relevant postgraduate courses through this entrance test.

Conducted by: Swami Vivekanand National Institute of Rehabilitation Training and Research (SVNIRTAR), Cuttack, Odisha.

📌 Quick Facts

Eligibility: Relevant Bachelor's degree with the required internship and marks, as applicable to the course

Mode: Offline examination

Questions: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

Total Marks: 100 marks

Duration: 90 minutes

Frequency: Conducted once a year

  • Qualification:
    • Must have completed a relevant Bachelor's degree from a recognized university.
    • The required degree depends on the postgraduate course, such as BPT, BOT, or BPO/B.Sc. in Prosthetics & Orthotics.
    • Required internship and other degree requirements must be completed within the time of applying.
  • Age:
    • There is no upper age limit specified for the SVNIRTAR PGET courses.
  • Minimum Marks:
    • Candidates must meet the minimum marks requirement specified for their chosen course.
    • For the SVNIRTAR courses, 50% marks are specified for MOT and MPO.
  • Attempts:
    • There is no limit on the number of attempts.

Mode:

  • The examination is conducted in offline mode.

Question Type:

  • The examination consists of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).

Total Questions:

  • There are 100 questions in total.

Duration:

  • The examination duration is 90 minutes.

Subjects:

  • Questions are based on the relevant Bachelor's degree-level syllabus.

Total Marks:

  • The examination carries 100 marks.

Marking Scheme:

  • 1 mark is awarded for each correct answer.
  • No negative marking for incorrect answers.

Postgraduate (PG) Courses:

  • Master of Physiotherapy (MPT)
  • Master of Occupational Therapy (MOT)
  • Master in Prosthetics & Orthotics (MPO)
  • M.Sc. Audiology

Note: Candidates should check the latest PGET notification before applying.
