Anaesthesia Technician

What Do They Do?

  • Imagine a patient has surgery today.
  • Before the surgery, anaesthesia has to be given to the patient.
  • The Anaesthesia Technician prepares the equipment and medicines needed to give anaesthesia.
  • During the surgery, they assist the anaesthesiologist.
  • After the surgery, they clean, check, and prepare the equipment for the next patient.
  • 👉 In simple words: An Anaesthesia Technician is a person who prepares the equipment and medicines needed to give anaesthesia and assists the anaesthesiologist during surgery.

🎓 How to Become an Anaesthesia Technician

The courses to pursue this career are given below :

After completing Class 12, you can pursue degree or diploma courses in anaesthesia technology to learn how to prepare anaesthesia equipment, assist the anaesthesia team, monitor patients, and support healthcare professionals during procedures.

Diploma in Anaesthesia Technology (DAT / DAOTT)

Duration: 2 Years

Eligibility: 10+2 Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology

Entrance Exams: State Paramedical Board Counseling / Institute-Level Entrance / Merit-Based Admission

B.Sc. in Anaesthesia Technology / Bachelor of Anaesthesia & Operation Theatre Technology (B.AOTT)

Duration: 3 to 4 Years (includes clinical internship)

Eligibility: 10+2 Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology

Entrance Exams: University/Institute Entrance Exams / State Paramedical or Allied Health Entrance Exams / Merit-Based Admission

B.Voc. in Anaesthesia & Critical Care Technology

Duration: 3 Years (includes practical and clinical training)

Eligibility: 10+2 Pass in Science Stream or equivalent

Entrance Exams: University Entrance Exams / CUET-UG / Merit-Based Admission

M.Sc. in Anaesthesia Technology

Duration: 2 Years

Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in Anaesthesia Technology / Anaesthesia & Operation Theatre Technology / Operation Theatre Technology from a recognized university (minimum marks may vary by institution)

Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in Anaesthesia & Operation Theatre Technology / Anaesthesia Technology / Operation Theatre Technology from a recognized university (minimum marks may vary by institution)
