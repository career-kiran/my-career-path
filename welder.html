Welder

What Do They Do?

  • A welder is someone who joins metal parts together using heat, electricity, or gas.
  • They cut and prepare metal parts before welding.
  • They use different welding machines and tools to join the metals.
  • They check the welded joints to make sure they are strong and properly joined.
  • 👉 In simple words: Welders join and repair metal parts and make sure the joints are strong and safe.

🎓 How to Become a Welder

There are different ways to become a welder.

You can start learning welding skills after Class 10 through an ITI course or other professional training programs.

You can also learn skills such as preparing metal parts, joining metals using different welding methods, handling welding equipment, and following safety rules by working with an experienced welder.

Further education such as a diploma or B.Voc in Welding Technology can help you build your skills and advance your career.

These welding programs help students learn welding methods, metal joining, welding tools, safety practices, and practical skills used in industries.
