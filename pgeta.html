PGETA (Postgraduate Entrance Test in Architecture)

PGETA (Postgraduate Entrance Test in Architecture) is a national-level entrance exam for students who want to study M.Arch. (Master of Architecture) in India.

Conducted by:Council of Architecture (CoA)

📌 Quick Facts

Eligibility: B.Arch. degree or equivalent qualification with at least 50% aggregate marks.

Mode of Exam: Computer-Based Test (CBT)

Total Questions: 100

Duration: 2 hours

Frequency: Conducted every year, with up to 3 test attempts

Qualification:

  • B.Arch.: Passed a B.Arch. degree or equivalent qualification.

Age: No age limit.

Minimum Marks:

  • All Candidates: At least 50% aggregate marks or equivalent CGPA.

Attempts:

  • Test 1: 1 attempt.
  • Test 2: 1 attempt.
  • Test 3: 1 attempt.

Note: Candidates can appear in up to 3 attempts. The best score is considered for the final evaluation.

Mode: Online (Computer-Based Test)

Question Type: Concept and skill-based questions

Duration: 2 hours (120 minutes)

Total Questions: 100

Modules:

  • Architecture & Design: 64 questions
  • Building Sciences & Applied Engineering: 14 questions
  • Professional Electives: 6 questions
  • Professional Ability & Skill Enhancement: 16 questions

PGETA is mainly used for admission to M.Arch. (Master of Architecture) programmes.

Course include:
  • M.Arch. (Master of Architecture) – 2 Years

Popular Specializations:

  • Architectural Design
  • Urban Design
  • Landscape Architecture
  • Architectural Conservation
  • Building Services
  • Housing
