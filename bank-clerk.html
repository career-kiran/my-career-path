Bank Clerk

👨‍💼 What Do They Do?

  • Imagine you are going to a bank to open a new account.
  • You may not know which form to fill out or what documents you need.
  • There is someone at the bank who helps you with these things.
  • They check your documents, help you fill out the form, and enter your details into the bank's system.
  • This person is a Bank Clerk.
  • 👉 In simple words: A Bank Clerk helps customers with their everyday banking work.

🎓 How to Become a Bank Clerk

  • There is no single course to become a Bank Clerk.
  • First you need to complete a bachelor's degree (graduation).
  • After graduation, you can apply for bank clerk recruitment exams or bank recruitment processes.
STEP 1

Pass Class 12 from any stream (Commerce, Science, or Arts).

STEP 2

Complete a bachelor's degree in any discipline

You can choose B.Com., BBA, B.A., B.Sc., B.Tech., or another recognised bachelor's degree.

STEP 3

Choose your entry route

  • Public Sector Banks
  • Regional Rural Banks
  • Private Banks
STEP 4

Public-sector banks usually recruit clerks through exams such as

SBI Clerk IBPS Clerk
STEP 5

Regional Rural Banks recruit clerical staff through

IBPS RRB Office Assistant
STEP 6

Private banks may recruit graduates through their own selection process, interviews, or training programmes.

STEP 7

Clear the selection process

Depending on the recruitment, you may need to clear an exam, interview, or other required stages.

STEP 8

After successfully completing the selection process, you can join in a bank as a Bank Clerk.

STEP 1

Imagine you are working as a Bank Clerk.

STEP 2

You start your day by getting the bank ready for customers.

STEP 3

Customers come to you for different things, such as opening an account, updating details, or depositing money.

STEP 4

You check their forms and documents and enter the required information.

STEP 5

You answer their questions and help them complete their banking work.

STEP 6

You also update records and handle other daily banking tasks.

STEP 7

At the end of the day, you make sure the work and records are properly completed.

STEP 8

If you enjoy helping customers, handling banking work, and working with documents, this career may suit you.
