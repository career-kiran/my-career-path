ICITSS Training

ICITSS (Integrated Course on Information Technology and Soft Skills) is a mandatory training program for CA students.

It helps students develop Information Technology skills, communication skills and other professional skills needed during their CA training and career.

ICITSS must be completed before starting Practical Training (Articleship).

What does ICITSS include?

ICITSS includes two parts:

  • Information Technology Training (ITT): Helps students develop the computer and technology skills needed for their CA training.
  • Orientation Course (OC): Helps students develop communication, presentation, teamwork and other professional skills.

Duration

  • Information Technology Training (ITT): 15 Days
  • Orientation Course (OC): 15 Days
  • Total Duration: 4 Weeks

Who needs to complete ICITSS?

    CA Intermediate students following either:
  • Foundation Route
  • Direct Entry Route
    • must complete ICITSS before starting their Practical Training.
