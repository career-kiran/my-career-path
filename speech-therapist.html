Speech Therapist

What Do They Do?

  • Imagine a child who has difficulty speaking clearly. The child's parents take the child to a speech therapy clinic.
  • The speech therapist first talks to the child and understands how the child speaks, understands, and communicates.
  • The therapist then plans certain activities to improve the child's speaking skills.
  • They also guide the parents on how to help the child improve speaking at home.
  • 👉 In simple words: A speech therapist helps people improve their speech and communication skills.

🎓 How to Become a Speech Therapist

The courses to pursue this career are given below :

After completing Class 12, you can pursue diploma or bachelor's degree courses in speech, language, and hearing to learn about communication disorders and their management.

Diploma in Hearing, Language and Speech (DHLS)

Duration: 1 Year

Eligibility: 12th Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics (Minimum 50% Marks)

Entrance Exam: Direct Admission / Institute-Level Entrance Exam (e.g., AIISH All India Entrance)

Bachelor of Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology (BASLP)

Duration: 4 Years (3 Years Academic + 1 Year Internship)

Eligibility: 12th Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics (Minimum 50% Marks)

Entrance Exam: AIISH Entrance Exam / CUET UG / State CETs / University Entrance Exams

After completing a relevant bachelor's degree, you can pursue postgraduate courses in speech-language pathology or audiology to develop advanced skills in communication and hearing care.

M.Sc. in Speech-Language Pathology (M.Sc. SLP) / MASLP

Duration: 2 Years

Eligibility: BASLP or B.Sc. in Speech and Hearing degree recognized by RCI (Minimum 50% Marks)

Entrance Exam: University PG Entrance Exams (e.g., AIISH All India Entrance, Manipal MET PG, State Medical University Exams)

Post Graduate Diploma in Auditory Verbal Therapy (PGDAVT)

Duration: 1 Year (2 Semesters)

Eligibility: BASLP / B.Sc. Speech & Hearing OR B.Ed. in Special Education (Hearing Impairment) with a minimum of 50% marks. RCI Registration is mandatory.

Entrance Exam: Institute / University PG Entrance Exam (e.g., Ali Yavar Jung National Institute Entrance Exam) or Direct Merit-Based Admission
