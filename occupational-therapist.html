Occupational Therapist

What Do They Do?

  • Imagine a person has injured their hand and finds it difficult to eat with a spoon.
  • The Occupational Therapist checks what is causing the difficulty.
  • They find that the person's hand is weak and difficult to move.
  • The therapist gives simple exercises and activities to improve the hand movement.
  • They guide the person and check their progress.
  • Slowly, the person gets better at using their hand and eating on their own.
  • 👉 In simple words: An Occupational Therapist helps people overcome difficulties in doing everyday activities.

Eligibility: 10+2 Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
