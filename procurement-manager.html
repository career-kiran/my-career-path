Procurement Manager

👨‍💼 What Do They Do?

  • Imagine a company needs to buy materials or products.
  • Someone has to find the right suppliers.
  • They compare prices, quality, and delivery times.
  • They talk to suppliers and negotiate prices.
  • They make sure the company gets what it needs on time.
  • That person is called a Procurement Manager.
  • 👉 In simple words: Procurement Managers make sure a company buys the right things at the right price from the right suppliers.
