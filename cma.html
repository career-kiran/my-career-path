Cost and Management Accountant (CMA)

What Do They Do?

  • Imagine you and your friend start a small business selling homemade cakes.
  • To make the cakes, you have to buy ingredients like flour, sugar, eggs, butter, and other things. You also need to spend money on packaging and other expenses.
  • You sell the cakes to your customers and start making money.
  • But you also have to spend money to buy all those ingredients, packaging, and other things.
  • So, how do you know whether you are actually making a profit?
  • A CMA studies your shop’s costs, spending, and other financial information. They help you reduce unnecessary spending and make better financial decisions.
  • 👉 In simple words: A CMA helps a business understand where its money is going, control its costs, and make better decisions.

How to Become a Cost and Management Accountant

  • A student can register for the CMA foundation course after passing Class 10, but can appear for the Foundation exam only after completing Class 12.
  • There are two main entry routes to join the Cost and Management Accountancy (CMA) program.
  • Pick the one that matches your current educational qualification
