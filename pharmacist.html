Pharmacist

What Do They Do?

  • You might have seen a pharmacy.
  • Imagine you go to a pharmacy with a prescription from a doctor. You give the prescription to the pharmacist.
  • The pharmacist checks the names of the medicines written on it and gives the medicines to you.
  • They also tell you how and when to take the medicines.
  • These people are called pharmacists.
  • 👉 In simple words: pharmacists study medicines and help people use them safely.

🎓 How to Become a Pharmacist

These pharmacy programs help students learn about medicines, drug preparation, how medicines work, patient care, and the safe use of medicines.

B.Pharm (Bachelor of Pharmacy)

Duration: 4 Years

Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics and Chemistry, along with Mathematics or Biology

Entrance Exam:

  • CUET-UG
  • State University Entrance Exams
  • University Entrance Exams

Pharm.D (Doctor of Pharmacy)

Duration: 6 Years (5 Years Study + 1 Year Internship)

Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics and Chemistry, along with Mathematics or Biology

Entrance Exam:

  • State-level Entrance Exams
  • Institute Entrance Exams

D.Pharm (Diploma in Pharmacy)

Duration: 2 Years

Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics and Chemistry, along with Mathematics or Biology

Entrance Exam:

  • State-level Entrance Exams
  • Merit-based Admission
