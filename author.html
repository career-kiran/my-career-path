Where do Authors work? (Job Roles + Growth)

Authors work in : Publishing companies, Book publishing houses, Newspapers and magazines, Media companies, Educational organisations, Digital publishing platforms, and as Independent or freelance writers.

🟢 Beginner Roles

Content Writer

After Certificate / Diploma

Writes online articles and blog posts for brands.

Junior Copywriter

After Diploma / B.A

Writes short text for social media and advertisements.

Creative Writing Intern

After 12th / Beginner Course

Helps proofread drafts and research story ideas.

🔵 Mid-Level Roles

Novelist / Author

After B.A / Writing Experience

Creates, writes, and publishes full-length books.

Senior Scriptwriter

After Media / Film Writing Experience

Writes scripts for videos, podcasts, and shows.

Narrative Designer

After Game Writing / Creative Writing

Writes stories and character dialogue for video games.

🟣 Advanced Roles

Chief Editor

After Journalism / Publishing Experience

Manages the writing team and approves final books.

Head of Content Strategy

After Marketing / Content Experience

Plans the brand's long-term goals for all written content.

Lead Ghostwriter

After Advanced Writing Experience

Writes books and speeches on behalf of famous people.
