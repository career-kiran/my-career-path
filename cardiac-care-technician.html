Cardiac Care Technician

What Do They Do?

  • A patient comes to the hospital with chest pain or breathing problems.
  • The doctor wants to check how the patient's heart is working.
  • A Cardiac Care Technician helps the doctor with the tests.
  • They set up special machines to check the heart.
  • They record the test results and share them with the doctor.
  • They also help the healthcare team during some heart procedures.
  • 👉 In simple words: A Cardiac Care Technician checks the heart using special machines and helps care for patients with heart problems.

🎓 How to Become a Cardiac Care Technician

The courses to pursue this career are given below :

After completing Class 12, you can pursue degree or diploma courses in cardiac care technology to learn how to assist in the diagnosis, monitoring, and treatment of patients with heart-related conditions.

Diploma in Cardiac Care Technology (DCCT)

Duration: 2 Years

Eligibility: 10+2 Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Minimum 45–50% Marks)

Entrance Exams: State Paramedical Board Counseling / University Entrance / Merit-Based Admission

B.Sc. in Cardiac Care Technology / Cardiovascular Technology

Duration: 3 to 4 Years (includes internship)

Eligibility: 10+2 Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Minimum 50% Marks)

Entrance Exams: University/Institute Entrance Exams / State Paramedical Entrance Exams / CUET-UG (where accepted) / Merit-Based Admission

B.Voc. in Cardiac Care Technology (Bachelor of Vocation)

Duration: 3 Years (includes practical clinical training)

Eligibility: 10+2 Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology / Science Stream (Minimum 45–50% Marks)

Entrance Exams: CUET-UG / University Entrance Exams / Direct Merit-Based Admission
