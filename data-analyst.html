Data Analyst

🧑‍🎨 What Do They Do?

  • Imagine a company notices low sales in some products.
  • The company wants to understand why?.
  • Some experts study data (sales, customer feedbacks and market trends) to find the reasons and suggest solutions
  • These professionals are called Data Analysts.
  • 👉 In simple words: Data Analysts study data and numbers to help companies make better decisions.

Where do Data Analysts work? (Job Roles + Growth)

Data Analyst work in : Technology companies, Financial Institutions, Consulting firms, Market Research companies, Healthcare Organisations, Retail and e-commerce companies, government organisations and other businesses.

🟢 Beginner Roles

Junior Data Analyst

(After B.Sc. / BCA / Certifications)

Cleans data and tracks business trends.

Research Assistant

(After B.A. / B.Sc. in Economics or Statistics)

Collects data and prepares reports.

Operations Analytics Trainee

(After 12th + Analytics Certificate)

Updates spreadsheets and tracks performance.

🔵 Mid-Level Roles

Senior Data Analyst

(Graduate Degree + Experience)

Builds dashboards and analyzes databases.

Market Research Analyst

(MBA / Master’s in Economics)

Studies customer and market trends.

Financial Data Analyst

(B.Com / Finance Degree)

Analyzes budgets and investment data.

🟣 Advanced Roles

Data Analytics Manager

(5+ Years Experience)

Leads analytics teams and business reporting.

BI Developer

(Advanced Certifications + Experience)

Designs company reporting systems.

Research Consultant

(Ph.D. / Advanced Master’s)

Advises organizations using research data.

Global Opportunities for Data Analysts

Data Analysts can work in finance, healthcare, technology, marketing, and business sectors worldwide.

Many countries hire data professionals to support business decisions and digital growth.

International Technology Companies

Global Banking & Finance Firms

E-Commerce Companies

Study customer behavior, sales performance, and online shopping trends.

Healthcare & Medical Organizations

Analyze healthcare records, patient data, and hospital performance.

International Consulting Firms

Help companies improve business strategies using analytics and reports.

Research & International Universities

Support academic research, surveys, and statistical data projects.
