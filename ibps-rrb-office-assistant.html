IBPS RRB Office Assistant

IBPS RRB Office Assistant (Multipurpose) is a recruitment exam conducted to select candidates for Office Assistant (Multipurpose) positions in participating Regional Rural Banks (RRBs).

The exam tests your skills in areas such as Reasoning, Numerical Ability, English/Hindi Language, General Awareness, and Computer Knowledge.

Example: A graduate who wants to start a career in banking through a Regional Rural Bank can appear for the IBPS RRB Office Assistant exam.

Conducted by: Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS).

📌 Quick Facts

Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university, along with proficiency in the local language and computer knowledge.

Exam Stages: Preliminary Examination and Main Examination, followed by provisional allotment.

Mode: Online examination.

Preliminary Exam: 80 questions, 80 marks, 45 minutes.

Main Exam: 200 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours.

Language: English/Hindi or the applicable regional language options, as specified by IBPS.

Frequency: Generally conducted once a year, subject to the IBPS recruitment calendar.

Qualification:

  • A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university or its equivalent.

Age:

  • Minimum age: 18 years.
  • Maximum age: 28 years.
  • Age relaxation is available for eligible categories as per IBPS rules.

Stream:

  • Graduation in any stream is accepted.

Computer Knowledge:

  • Working knowledge of computers is desirable.

Local Language:

  • Candidates must be proficient in the specified local language of the State or Union Territory they apply for.

Min marks:

  • No minimum percentage is specified in graduation.

Attempts:

  • There is no separate fixed limit on the number of attempts.
  • You can apply as long as you meet the age and other eligibility requirements.

Mode: Online

Phase 1: Preliminary Examination

  • Reasoning: 40 questions, 40 marks
  • Numerical Ability: 40 questions, 40 marks
  • Total: 80 questions, 80 marks, 45 minutes (composite time)

Phase 2: Main Examination

  • Reasoning: 40 questions, 50 marks
  • Computer Knowledge: 40 questions, 20 marks
  • General Awareness: 40 questions, 40 marks
  • English Language: 40 questions, 40 marks
  • Hindi Language: 40 questions, 40 marks
  • Numerical Ability: 40 questions, 50 marks
  • Total: 200 questions, 200 marks, 2 hours (composite time)

Language Choice: Candidates can choose either English Language or Hindi Language in the Main Examination.

Negative Marking:

  • 1/4th of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer in the objective tests.

Stage 1: Preliminary Examination

  • Candidates must qualify in the Preliminary Examination to be shortlisted for the Main Examination.

Stage 2: Main Examination

  • Candidates who qualify in the Preliminary Examination and are shortlisted will appear for the Main Examination.

Stage 3: Provisional Allotment

  • Candidates are provisionally allotted to a participating Regional Rural Bank based on their marks in the Main Examination, vacancies, merit, reservation rules, and other applicable conditions.

Final Appointment

  • Appointment is subject to fulfilment of eligibility criteria, document verification, identity verification, and completion of other requirements specified by the allotted Regional Rural Bank.

Note: Public sector banks may conduct their own recruitment or recruit through common banking examinations such as IBPS, depending on the post and recruitment notification.
