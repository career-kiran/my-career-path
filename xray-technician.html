X-Ray Technician

What Do They Do?

  • Imagine a patient visits the hospital with a complaint of chest pain and difficulty in breathing.
  • The doctor examines him and advises an X-ray to check his lungs.
  • The patient is sent to the X-ray room.
  • An X-Ray Technician will be there in the X-ray room.
  • They position the patient correctly in front of the X-ray machine and take the X-ray.
  • They also make sure the X-ray image is clear and properly captured.
  • 👉 In simple words: X-Ray Technicians operate the X-ray machine and take X-ray images.

After completing Class 12, you can pursue diploma or degree courses in X-ray, radiography, and medical imaging technology to learn how to operate X-ray equipment and take medical X-ray images.

Diploma in X-Ray Technology

Duration: 2 Years

Eligibility: 12th Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics (Minimum 40%–50% Marks)

Entrance Exam: State Paramedical Board Entrance Exams / Merit-Based

B.Sc. in Medical Radio Imaging Technology (BMRIT) / BRIT

Duration: 3 to 4 Years (Includes Internship)

Eligibility: 12th Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Minimum 50% Marks)

Entrance Exam: State Paramedical Exam / University-Level Entrance Exams (e.g., AIIMS, KGMU)

B.Sc. in Radiography

Duration: 3 to 4 Years (Often includes 1-year clinical internship)

Eligibility: 12th Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics (Minimum 50% Marks)

Entrance Exam: State-Level CETs / University Entrance Exams (e.g., AIIMS, KGMU, Manipal MET) / Merit-Based

B.Voc in Medical Imaging Technology

Duration: 3 Years

Eligibility: 12th Pass in Science stream (Some skill universities accept 12th pass from any stream or 3-year Polytechnic Diploma)

Entrance Exam: University-Level Entrance Exams / Merit-Based Admission
