Optometrist

What Do They Do?

  • A person visits an eye clinic to get their eyes checked.
  • Before seeing the doctor, a person checks their eyes and asks to read the letters on a chart on the wall.
  • They perform different tests to check the power of the eyes and find out if the person has any vision or eye problems.
  • The person who does these eye tests is called an optometrist.
  • 👉 In simple words: An optometrist checks eyesight, identifies vision problems, prescribes glasses or contact lenses, and helps people take care of their eyes.

🎓 How to Become an Optometrist

The courses to pursue this career are given below :

After completing Class 12, you can pursue diploma or bachelor's degree courses in optometry to learn about eye care, vision testing, and correcting common vision problems.

Diploma in Optometry (D.Opt / D.Optom)

Duration: 2 Years

Eligibility: 12th Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics (Minimum 45%–50% Marks)

Entrance Exam: State Paramedical Board Entrance Exams / Merit-Based

Bachelor of Optometry (B.Optom / B.Sc. Optometry)

Duration: 4 Years (3 Years Academic + 1 Year Clinical Internship)

Eligibility: 12th Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics (Minimum 50% Marks)

Entrance Exam: CUET UG, State-Level CETs (e.g., KCET, GUJCET), or University Entrance Exams (e.g., AIIMS, Manipal MET)
