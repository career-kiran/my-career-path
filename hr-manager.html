H R Manager

🧑‍🎨 What Do They Do?

  • Imagine a company has many employees doing different jobs.
  • HR manager help the company hire people when new employees are needed.
  • They helps employees solve problems, communicate better, and work well together.
  • They help new employees understand their jobs and arrange training to improve their skills.
  • 👉 In simple words: HR Managers take care of the people working in a company.

🎓 How to Become an HR Manager

The courses to pursue this career are given below :

  • After completing 12th, you can pursue a bachelor's degree in human resources, business administration.
  • You can then start your career in an entry-level HR role and gain experience and become an HR Manager.
