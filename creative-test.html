Creative Test

Creative Test is a test conducted by some colleges and training institutes to assess the creative skills of a student.

It is mainly conducted before giving admission to creative courses such as Animation, Graphic Design, Fashion Design, Interior Design, Fine Arts, and similar programmes.

This test evaluates a student's creativity, imagination, observation skills, and creative thinking.

Example:

A student applies for a Diploma in 3D Animation.

The college or institute may ask the student to draw a simple object or complete a creative activity.

Based on the student's performance, the institute decides whether to offer admission or not.
