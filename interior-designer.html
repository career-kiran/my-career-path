Interior Designer

What Do They Do?

  • Imagine you are building a new home.
  • You want your home to look good.
  • You need to decide how each room should look, where to place the furniture and lights.
  • An interior designer helps you plan each room.
  • They choose colours, furniture, lights, and other materials.
  • They also make a drawing to show how the room will look.
  • 👉 In simple words: Interior Designers plan and design the inside of a home.
