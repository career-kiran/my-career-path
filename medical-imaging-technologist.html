Medical Imaging Technologist

What Do They Do?

  • A boy falls from his bicycle. He has pain in his arm, so his parents take him to the hospital.
  • The doctor checks him and wants to know whether his arm is broken. So, the doctor sends him for an X-ray.
  • In the X-ray room, there is a person who positions him correctly and takes an X-ray of his arm.
  • They also check whether the X-ray image is clear and properly captured.
  • The person who does this work is called a Medical Imaging Technologist.
  • 👉 In simple words: Medical Imaging Technologists are healthcare professionals who operate medical imaging equipment such as X-ray, CT scan, and MRI

🎓 How to Become a Medical Imaging Technologist

The courses to pursue this career are given below :

After completing 12th, you can pursue diploma or degree courses in medical imaging technology to learn how to operate imaging equipment and create images of the inside of the body.

Diploma in Medical Radiology and Imaging Technology (DMRIT)

Duration: 2.5 Years (including clinical training/internship)

Eligibility: 10+2 with Science subjects

Entrance Exam: State Paramedical Entrance Exam / Institute-Specific Entrance Test / Merit-Based Admission

Bachelor of Medical Radiology and Imaging Technology (BMRIT)

Duration: 3–4 Years (including internship)

Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology

Entrance Exam: AIIMS B.Sc. Allied & Health Care Entrance Examination / CUET-UG / State Paramedical Entrance Exam / Institute-Specific Entrance Test / Merit-Based Admission

B.Sc. in Medical Imaging Technology (B.Sc. MIT)

Duration: 3–4 Years (including internship)

Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology

Entrance Exam: CUET-UG / State Paramedical Entrance Exam / Institute-Specific Entrance Test / Merit-Based Admission

B.Sc. in Radiography and Medical Imaging Technology

Duration: 3–4 Years (including internship)

Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology

Entrance Exam: CUET-UG / State Paramedical Entrance Exam / Institute-Specific Entrance Test / Merit-Based Admission
