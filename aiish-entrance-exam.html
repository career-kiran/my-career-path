AIISH Entrance Examination

AIISH Entrance Examination is an entrance examination conducted for admission to selected programmes in speech, language, and hearing sciences.

The examination is mainly for students who want to study courses such as Bachelor of Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology (B.ASLP), M.Sc. Audiology, and M.Sc. Speech-Language Pathology, depending on the admission session.

Conducted by: All India Institute of Speech and Hearing (AIISH), Mysuru.

📌 Quick Facts

Eligibility: Class 12 (for B.ASLP) or relevant Bachelor’s degree (for M.Sc. programmes)

Mode: Computer Based Test (CBT)

Questions: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

Duration: 150 Minutes (B.ASLP) / 90 Minutes (M.Sc.)

Frequency: Usually conducted once a year

B.ASLP

  • Qualification: Passed Class 12 or equivalent with the required subjects.
  • Minimum Marks: 50% in the required subjects. 45% for SC/ST candidates.
  • Age: As per the latest AIISH admission rules.
  • Attempts: No fixed limit.

M.Sc. Audiology

  • Qualification: B.ASLP or an equivalent Bachelor’s degree in Speech and Hearing.
  • Minimum Marks: As per the latest AIISH admission rules.
  • Age: As per the latest AIISH admission rules.
  • Attempts: No fixed limit.

M.Sc. Speech-Language Pathology

  • Qualification: B.ASLP or an equivalent Bachelor’s degree in Speech and Hearing.
  • Minimum Marks: As per the latest AIISH admission rules.
  • Age: As per the latest AIISH admission rules.
  • Attempts: No fixed limit.

B.ASLP

  • Mode: Computer Based Test (CBT)
  • Questions: 150 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • Duration: 150 Minutes
  • Subjects: Physics, Chemistry and Biology/Mathematics
  • Total Marks: 150 Marks
  • Correct Answer: +1 Mark
  • Wrong Answer: 0 Marks
  • Unanswered: 0 Marks

M.Sc. Audiology

  • Mode: Computer Based Test (CBT)
  • Questions: 100 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • Duration: 100 Minutes
  • Total Marks: 100 Marks
  • Correct Answer: +1 Mark
  • Wrong Answer: −0.25 Mark
  • Unanswered: 0 Marks

M.Sc. Speech-Language Pathology

  • Mode: Computer Based Test (CBT)
  • Questions: 100 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • Duration: 100 Minutes
  • Total Marks: 100 Marks
  • Correct Answer: +1 Mark
  • Wrong Answer: −0.25 Mark
  • Unanswered: 0 Marks

After clearing the AIISH Entrance Examination, students can get admission to the following courses, depending on the programme they apply for:

  • B.ASLP – Bachelor of Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology
  • M.Sc. Audiology
  • M.Sc. Speech-Language Pathology (M.Sc. SLP)

Note: The courses offered may vary depending on the admission session.
