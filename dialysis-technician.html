Dialysis Technologist / Technician

What Do They Do?

  • Some people with serious kidney problems need dialysis to clean their blood.
  • The dialysis technologist / technician prepares the dialysis machine before the treatment.
  • They connect the patient to the dialysis machine and start the treatment.
  • During dialysis, they watch the patient and the machine to make sure everything is going well.
  • They keep the equipment clean and safe to prevent infections.
  • 👉 In simple words: A dialysis technologist / technician operates the dialysis machine and helps take care of patients during dialysis treatment.
