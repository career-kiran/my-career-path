Medical Laboratory Technologist

What Do They Do?

  • Imagine a person is admitted to a hospital because they are not feeling well.
  • A blood sample is collected and sent to the laboratory for testing.
  • In the laboratory, someone performs different tests, examines the samples, and shares the findings with the medical team.
  • The person who performs this laboratory work is called a Medical Laboratory Technologist.
  • 👉 In simple words: They test blood, urine, and other samples in medical laboratories.

🎓 How to Become a Medical Laboratory Technologist

The courses to pursue this career are given below :

After completing 12th, you can pursue diploma or degree courses in medical laboratory technology to build the knowledge and practical skills needed to work in medical laboratories.

Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology (DMLT)

Duration: 2 Years

Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology

Entrance Exam: State Paramedical Entrance Exams / Institute Specific Test / Merit-based admission

B.Sc. in Medical Laboratory Technology (B.Sc. MLT)

Duration: 3–4 Years (including internship)

Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology (min mark varies by college)

Entrance Exam: CUET-UG / State Paramedical Entrance Exam / Institute-Specific Test / Merit-Based Admission
