AIIMS M.Sc. Entrance Examination

AIIMS M.Sc. Entrance Examination is an entrance examination conducted for admission to various M.Sc. programmes offered by AIIMS.

The examination is mainly for students who have completed a relevant Bachelor’s degree and want to study a postgraduate M.Sc. course in medical, health, or related subjects.

Conducted by: All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi.

📌 Quick Facts

Eligibility: Relevant Bachelor’s degree

Mode: Computer Based Test (CBT)

Duration: 90 Minutes

Questions: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

Frequency: Usually conducted once a year

Qualification: A relevant Bachelor’s degree from a recognized university. Eligibility requirements may be different for different M.Sc. courses.

Minimum Marks: The minimum marks required may vary depending on the M.Sc. course and category.

Age: There is generally no upper age limit.

Attempts: There is no common limit on the number of attempts.

Mode: Computer Based Test (CBT)

Duration: 90 Minutes

Total Questions: 90 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

Total Marks: 90 Marks

Correct Answer: +1 Mark

Incorrect Answer: −1/3 Mark

Unanswered Questions: 0 Marks

After clearing the AIIMS M.Sc. Entrance Examination, students can get admission to M.Sc. programmes offered by AIIMS, depending on the course and available seats.

Courses include:-
  • M.Sc. Medical Anatomy
  • M.Sc. Medical Biochemistry
  • M.Sc. Biophysics
  • M.Sc. Medical Physiology
  • M.Sc. Medical Pharmacology
  • M.Sc. Reproductive Biology and Clinical Embryology
  • M.Sc. Nuclear Medicine Technology
  • M.Sc. Perfusion Technology

Note: The courses offered may vary depending on the admission session.
