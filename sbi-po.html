SBI PO

SBI PO (State Bank of India Probationary Officer) is a recruitment exam conducted by the State Bank of India (SBI) to select candidates for the Probationary Officer position.

The exam tests your skills in areas such as English, Mathematics, Reasoning, and General Awareness.

Example: A graduate who wants to start a career as a bank officer in SBI can appear for the SBI PO exam.

Conducted by: State Bank of India (SBI).

📌 Quick Facts

Eligibility: Bachelor's degree from a recognised university.

Exam Pattern: Preliminary Exam and Main Exam, followed by further selection stages.

Mode: Online.

Preliminary Exam: 100 questions, 100 marks, 1 hour.

Main Exam: Objective and descriptive tests.

Conducted by: State Bank of India (SBI).

Qualification:

  • A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university.
  • Final-year students can also apply, subject to the conditions in the official notification.

Age:

  • Minimum age: 21 years.
  • Maximum age: 30 years.
  • Age relaxation is available for eligible categories as per SBI rules.

Stream:

  • Graduation in any stream is accepted.

Min mark:

  • No minimum percentage is required in graduation.

Attempts:

  • General / EWS: 4 attempts.
  • OBC / PwBD: 7 attempts.
  • SC / ST: No restriction.
  • Appearing in the Preliminary Exam does not count as an attempt.

Mode: Online

Phase 1: Preliminary Examination

  • English Language: 40 questions, 40 marks, 20 minutes
  • Quantitative Aptitude: 30 questions, 30 marks, 20 minutes
  • Reasoning Ability: 30 questions, 30 marks, 20 minutes
  • Total: 100 questions, 100 marks, 1 hour

Phase 2: Main Examination – Objective Test

  • Reasoning & Computer Aptitude: 40 questions, 60 marks, 50 minutes
  • Data Analysis & Interpretation: 30 questions, 60 marks, 45 minutes
  • General Awareness / Economy / Banking Knowledge: 60 questions, 60 marks, 45 minutes
  • English Language: 40 questions, 20 marks, 40 minutes
  • Total: 170 questions, 200 marks, 3 hours

Phase 2: Main Examination – Descriptive Test

  • Total Marks: 50 marks
  • Duration: 30 minutes
  • Language: English
  • Topics: Email writing, situation analysis, report writing and précis writing

Phase 3: Final Selection

  • Psychometric Test
  • Group Exercise: 20 marks
  • Personal Interview: 30 marks

Negative Marking:

  • 1/4th of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer in the objective tests.

Phase 1: Preliminary Examination

Candidates must qualify in the Preliminary Examination to move to the Main Examination.

Phase 2: Main Examination

Candidates who qualify in the Main Examination are shortlisted for Phase 3.

Phase 3: Psychometric Test, Group Exercise & Personal Interview

  • Psychometric Test: Assesses personality traits and behavioural characteristics.
  • Group Exercise: Assesses a candidate's ability to work and communicate in a group.
  • Personal Interview: Assesses the candidate's suitability for the role of a Probationary Officer.

Final Selection:

Final selection is based on the candidate's performance in the Main Examination and Phase 3, along with fulfilment of the required eligibility and other conditions.
