NATA (National Aptitude Test in Architecture)

NATA (National Aptitude Test in Architecture) is a national-level aptitude test for students who want to study B.Arch. (Bachelor of Architecture) in India.

The test checks skills such as drawing, visual understanding, logical reasoning, critical thinking and design sensitivity.

Conducted by: Council of Architecture (CoA)

📌 Quick Facts

Eligibility: Students who have passed or are appearing for 10+2 with the required subjects, or 10+3 Diploma with Mathematics.

Mode of Exam:

  • Part A – Offline
  • Part B – Computer-Based Adaptive Test

Total Questions:

  • 53 questions — 3 Drawing & Composition questions
  • 50 MCQ/NCQ questions

Duration: 3 hours (90 minutes for Part A + 90 minutes for Part B)

Total Marks: 200 marks

Frequency: Conducted every year, with multiple exam dates

Qualification:

  • 10+2: Passed or appearing in 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with one of the subjects prescribed by the Council of Architecture.
  • 10+3 Diploma: Passed or appearing in a 10+3 Diploma with Mathematics as a compulsory subject.

Age: No age limit.

Minimum Marks:

  • 10+2: At least 45% marks in aggregate.
  • 10+3 Diploma: At least 45% marks in aggregate.

Attempts:

  • Phase 1: Up to 2 attempts.
  • Phase 2: 1 attempt. Only for candidates who did not appear in phase 1.

Note:

  • Qualifying NATA does not guarantee admission.
  • Candidates must also meet the eligibility requirements of the university or admission authority.

Mode: Part A – Offline; Part B – Online Computer-Based Adaptive Test

Duration: 3 hours (90 minutes for Part A + 90 minutes for Part B)

Total Questions: 53

Total Marks: 200 marks

Part A: Drawing and Composition – 3 questions, 80 marks

  • A1 – Composition and Color: 25 marks
  • A2 – Sketching and Composition: 25 marks
  • A3 – 3D Composition: 30 marks

Part B: 50 questions, 120 marks

  • 42 MCQs: Multiple Choice Questions
  • 8 NCQs: Numerical Choice Questions

NCQ: Answer of the questions will be a 'number'(eg. 1,2,3...etc).

Topics in Part B:

  • Visual Reasoning
  • Logical Derivation
  • General Knowledge, Architecture & Design
  • Language Interpretation
  • Design Sensitivity and Thinking
  • Numerical Ability

NATA is mainly used for admission to the B.Arch. (Bachelor of Architecture) course.

Course include:
  • B.Arch. (Bachelor of Architecture) – 5 years

What you can study in B.Arch.:

  • Architectural Design
  • Building Construction
  • Building Services
  • History of Architecture
  • Urban Design and Planning
  • Landscape Design
