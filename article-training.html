Articleship Training

Articleship Training is a mandatory practical training program for CA students.

Students will be working under a practicing Chartered Accountant and learn practical experience in accounting, auditing, taxation and other CA-related work.

Articleship Training is for 2 years under the current CA course scheme.

Who can start Articleship?

You can start Articleship after passing both groups of the CA Intermediate examination and completing the required ICITSS training.

What do you do during Articleship?

During Articleship, you work on real accounting and finance-related tasks under the guidance of a Chartered Accountant.

  • Help with accounting and financial records.
  • Assist with audits.
  • Work on taxation-related tasks.
  • Learn how businesses follow financial rules and regulations.

Duration

You need to complete 2 years of practical training as part of the CA course.
