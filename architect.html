Architect

What Do They Do?

  • Imagine a family wants to build a new house.
  • For building a house, you need a proper plan — how many rooms there should be, how much space each room needs, where the kitchen and bathrooms should go and more.
  • So, the family meets an Architect.
  • The architect understands what the family needs and draws the plan for the house.
  • The architect then visits the construction site and checks the work to make sure it is being built according to the plan.
  • 👉 In simple words: Architects plan buildings and guide their construction.

🎓 How to Become an Architect

B.Arch programs help students learn about design, drawing, planning, construction, and how to create safe and useful spaces for people.

B.Arch. (Bachelor of Architecture)

Duration: 5 Years

Eligibility: 12th with Physics and Mathematics and at least 45% aggregate marks, or 10+3 Diploma with Mathematics and at least 45% marks

Entrance Exam:

  • NATA
  • JEE Main (Paper 2A)
  • JEE Advanced AAT
