Advanced ICITSS

Advanced ICITSS (Advanced Integrated Course on Information Technology and Soft Skills) is a mandatory training program for CA students.

It helps students develop advanced Information Technology skills, communication skills and other professional skills needed for their CA career.

Advanced ICITSS must be completed after Practical Training and before appearing for the CA Final examination.

What does Advanced ICITSS include?

Advanced ICITSS includes two training programs:

  • Advanced IT Training: Students learn advanced computer and technology skills. After completing the training, they must pass the Advanced IT Test conducted by ICAI.
  • Management and Communication Skills (MCS) Training: Students develop communication, leadership, teamwork and other professional skills.

Duration

  • Advanced IT Training: 15 Days
  • Management and Communication Skills (MCS) Training: 15 Days
  • Total Duration: 4 Weeks

When should you complete Advanced ICITSS?

Students must complete Advanced ICITSS after completing their Practical Training and before appearing for the CA Final examination.
