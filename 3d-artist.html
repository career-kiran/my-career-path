3D Artist

🧑‍🎨 What Do They Do?

  • When you play a video game or watch an animated movie...
  • You see many characters and objects
  • Someone uses software to create them before they appear on the screen
  • The person who does this is called a 3D Artist.
  • 👉 In simple words: 3D artists create digital characters and objects in animation and video games.

🎓 How to Become a 3D Artist

The courses to pursue this career are given below :

Where do 3D Artists work? (Job Roles + Growth)

3D Artist work in : Animation and VFX studios, Film and Television production companies, Gaming companies, Advertising agencies, Architecture and visualization firms, Design studios, Virtual production companies and other Entertainment companies.

🟢 Beginner Roles

Junior 3D Modeler

(After Diploma / B.Voc)

Works in gaming studios or advertising companies.

3D Texture Artist

(After B.Sc / Certificate)

Works in visualization or film production.

3D Lighting Artist

After Diploma in VFX

Works in animation or architectural firms.

3D Scanning Technician

After Certificate / B.Sc

Works in e-commerce or heritage projects.

🔵 Mid-Level Roles

3D Character Artist

After B.Sc / B.Des + Experience

Works in game or film studios.

Environment Artist

After B.Des / B.Voc + Experience

Works in gaming studios or virtual productions.

Look Development Artist

After B.Sc / M.Sc

Works in VFX studios.

Hard Surface Modeler

After Diploma + Specialization

Works in industries or sci-fi design studios.

🟣 Advanced Roles

Asset Lead

After Graduation + Experience

Manages asset creation team and ensures quality standards.

Technical Artist

After B.Tech / B.Sc

Works between coding and art.

Art Director

After BFA / B.Des

Decides overall visual style and direction of projects.

International Animation Studios

Film and VFX Studios

Freelance and Remote Work
