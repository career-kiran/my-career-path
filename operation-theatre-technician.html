Operation Theatre Technician

What Do They Do?

  • Imagine a patient is admitted to the hospital and needs surgery.
  • Before the surgery, the Operation Theatre Technician arranges all the surgical instruments and equipment needed for the surgery.
  • During the surgery, they assist the surgical team.
  • After the surgery, they help to clean and prepare the operation theatre for the next patient.
  • 👉 In simple words: An Operation Theatre Technician prepares the operation theatre, arranges the equipment, and assists the surgical team during surgery.

🎓 How to Become a Operation Theatre Technician

The courses to pursue this career are given below :

After completing Class 12, you can pursue degree or diploma courses in operation theatre technology to learn how to prepare the operation theatre, handle medical equipment, maintain sterile conditions, and assist the surgical team during procedures.

Diploma in Operation Theatre Technology (DOTT)

Duration: 2 Years

Eligibility: 10+2 Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology

Entrance Exams: State Paramedical Board Counseling / University Entrance / Merit-Based Admission

B.Sc. in Operation Theatre Technology / Bachelor in Anaesthesia & Operation Theatre Technology

Duration: 3 to 4 Years (includes internship)

Eligibility: 10+2 Pass with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology

Entrance Exams: University Entrance Exams / State Paramedical or Allied Health Entrance Exams / CUET-UG (where accepted) / Merit-Based Admission

B.Voc. in Operation Theatre Technology (Bachelor of Vocation)

Duration: 3 Years (includes practical and clinical training)

Eligibility: 10+2 Pass or equivalent

Entrance Exams: CUET-UG / University Entrance Exams / Merit-Based Admission
