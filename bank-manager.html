Bank Manager

What Do They Do?

  • When you visit a bank, you might have seen different people working there.
  • One person handling loans, another person working in the cash section, and others handling different banking activities.
  • So, there are many things happening inside a bank.
  • Someone needs to make sure that everything is working properly.
  • That person is called a Bank Manager.
  • 👉 In simple words: A Bank Manager makes sure that the bank is functioning properly and all customers are getting the help they need.

🎓 How to Become a Bank Manager

  • There is no single course to become a Bank Manager.
  • First you need to complete a bachelor's degree (graduation).
  • After graduation, you can enter the banking sector as a Probationary Officer (PO) or a Clerk (LDC) by clearing the selection process..
  • When you get experience, you will get promotion and becomes a bank manager.
STEP 1

Pass Class 12 from any stream (Commerce, Science, or Arts).

STEP 2

Complete a bachelor's degree in any stream

You can choose B.Com., BBA, B.A., B.Sc., B.Tech., or another recognised bachelor's degree.

STEP 3

Enter the banking sector

You can apply for bank officer jobs in public-sector or private-sector banks.

STEP 4

Choose your entry route

  • Public Sector Banks
  • Private Banks
  • Regional Rural Banks
STEP 5

Public-sector banks usually recruit officers through exams such as SBI PO IBPS PO

STEP 6

Private banks may recruit officers through their own selection process, interviews, or training programmes.

STEP 7

Regional Rural Banks recruit officers through IBPS RRB exam

IBPS RRB
STEP 8

Gain experience in banking

Learn how to handle customers, loans, accounts, employees, and daily bank work.

STEP 9

With experience and promotion, you can become a Bank Manager

STEP 1

Imagine you are working as a Bank Manager in a bank.

STEP 2

You start your day by checking the work planned for the day and making sure the bank is ready to serve customers.

STEP 3

You supervise the employees working in different sections, such as cash, loans, accounts, and customer service.

STEP 4

You help handle important customer issues and make decisions about banking services, loans, and other matters.

STEP 5

You check the bank's daily work, make sure rules are followed, and solve problems when they arise.

STEP 6

You also guide your team and make sure the branch is working smoothly.

STEP 7

If you enjoy managing people, solving problems, and helping customers, this career may suit you.
