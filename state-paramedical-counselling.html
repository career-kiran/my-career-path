State Paramedical Counselling

State Paramedical Counselling is an admission process that helps students get seats in paramedical and allied healthcare courses.

Counselling is the process of getting a seat in a course and college.

In some states, students need to write an entrance exam before counselling. In other states, students can directly take part in counselling based on their eligibility or merit.

During counselling, students can choose their preferred course and college. A seat is allotted based on factors such as merit, eligibility, reservation rules, and available seats.

What Do Students Do During Counselling?

  • Register for counselling.
  • Choose their preferred course and college.
  • Wait for the seat allotment.
  • Get a seat based on their merit and available seats.
  • Complete the admission if they accept the seat.

What is Seat Allotment?

Seat allotment is when the counselling authority gives a student a seat in a course and college based on the student's merit, choices, eligibility, and available seats.

In simple words: Seat allotment means deciding which course and college the student gets.

Example: How Counselling Works

Suppose a student has completed Class 12 and wants to study B.Sc. Medical Laboratory Technology (B.Sc. MLT).

First, the student registers for counselling.

During counselling, the student chooses B.Sc. MLT and Government Medical College, Kozhikode as the preferred college.

The counselling authority checks the student's merit and whether the student is eligible to get admission to the Government Medical College, Kozhikode.

If the student is eligible, the authority then checks whether a seat is available in B.Sc. MLT at the chosen college.

If a seat is available and the student is allotted that seat and student can complete the admission process.

In simple words:

Register → Choose Course & College → Eligibility & Merit Check → Seat Availability → Seat Allotment → Admission
