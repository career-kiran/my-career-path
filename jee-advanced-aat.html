JEE Advanced AAT

AAT (Architecture Aptitude Test) is an entrance test for students who want to study B.Arch. (Bachelor of Architecture) at IITs.

It is conducted for admission to the B.Arch. programme at IIT (BHU) Varanasi, IIT Kharagpur and IIT Roorkee.

Only candidates who have qualified JEE Advanced can appear for AAT.

📌 Quick Facts

Eligibility: Candidates who have qualified JEE Advanced

Mode of Exam: Offline (Pen-and-Paper)

Total Questions: Not specified in the official brochure

Duration: 3 hours

Frequency: Conducted every year (usually June)

Qualification:

  • JEE Advanced: Must have qualified JEE Advanced 2026.

Age: No separate age limit for AAT. Candidates must meet the age eligibility requirements of JEE Advanced.

Minimum Marks:

  • JEE Advanced: Must qualify JEE Advanced 2026.
  • Class 12: Candidates must also satisfy the Class 12 requirements for B.Arch. admission.

Attempts: AAT can be taken once in the year.

Note: Candidates must register separately for AAT after the JEE Advanced results.

Mode: Offline (Pen-and-Paper)

Duration: 3 hours

Total Questions: 16

Total Marks: 300 marks

Sections:

  • Section A – Architectural Awareness: 4 questions, 60 marks
  • Section B – Geometrical Drawing: 3 questions, 60 marks
  • Section C – 3D Perception: 3 questions, 60 marks
  • Section D – Imagination & Aesthetic Sensitivity: 3 questions, 60 marks
  • Section E – Freehand Drawing: 3 questions, 60 marks

Marking Scheme: No negative marking.

Note: The number of questions, marks and exam pattern may change in future years. Check the official JEE Advanced website for the latest details.

AAT is used for admission to the B.Arch. (Bachelor of Architecture) programme at selected IITs.

Course include:
  • B.Arch. (Bachelor of Architecture) – 5 Years

IITs Offering B.Arch.:

  • IIT (BHU) Varanasi
  • IIT Kharagpur
  • IIT Roorkee
