NIPER JEE (NIPER Joint Entrance Examination)

NIPER JEE (NIPER Joint Entrance Examination) is a national-level entrance exam for students who want to study postgraduate and doctoral programs in pharmaceutical sciences at the National Institutes of Pharmaceutical Education and Research (NIPERs).

The exam is conducted by NIPER for admission to programs such as M.S. (Pharm.), M.Pharm., M.Tech., M.B.A. (Pharm.) and Ph.D., depending on the programme and eligibility requirements.

📌 Quick Facts

Eligibility: Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy or a relevant degree, depending on the programme.

Mode of Exam: Computer Based Test (CBT)

Total Questions: 200 Multiple Choice Questions

Duration: 150 minutes

Frequency: Conducted once a year (usually June/July)

Qualification: A relevant bachelor’s or master’s degree from a recognized university, depending on the programme.

Candidates must also have a valid GPAT, GATE, NET, or other National Fellowship score, depending upon the course.

Age: No upper age limit.

Minimum Marks:

  • General / OBC / EWS: At least 60% aggregate marks or CGPA 6.75 / 10
  • SC / ST: At least 55% aggregate marks or CGPA 6.25 / 10
  • PwBD: At least 50% aggregate marks or CGPA 5.75 / 10

Attempts: No limit on the number of attempts.

Mode: Online (Computer-Based Test)

Question Type: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

Duration: 150 minutes (2 hours 30 minutes)

Frequency: Conducted once a year

Total Questions: 200

Section A: 80 questions

Section B: 120 questions

Marking Scheme:

  • +0.5 – for every correct answer
  • −0.125 – for every wrong answer
  • 0 – for an unanswered question

Through NIPER JEE, qualified candidates can get admission to postgraduate programmes in pharmaceutical sciences at NIPER institutes.

Courses include:
  • M.S. (Pharm.)
  • M.Pharm.
  • M.Tech. (Pharm.)
  • M.B.A. (Pharm.)

Specializations include:

  • Medicinal Chemistry
  • Natural Products
  • Traditional Medicine
  • Pharmaceutical Analysis
  • Pharmacology and Toxicology
  • Regulatory Toxicology
  • Pharmaceutics
  • Biotechnology
  • Pharmacoinformatics
  • Pharmacy Practice and Clinical Research
  • Pharmaceutical Technology (Formulations)
  • Pharmaceutical Technology (Process Chemistry)
  • Pharmaceutical Technology (Biotechnology)
  • Pharmaceutical Management
